---

Shows the queue of the currently active media player. NextCP/2 supports native OpenHome playlists as
well as a self managed queue for regular UPnP devices without internal playlist support.

The line under the title says what is queued, how long it runs and where it plays — for example
*12 tracks, 48 min on Living Room*.

## The list

Every row carries its position in the queue, the cover, title and artist — album, the quality badge of
the track and its playing time. Pointing at a row turns the position into a
<span class="material-symbols-outlined">play_arrow</span>: click anywhere on the row to start there,
and playback continues with the following tracks. The track that is playing is highlighted and shows
a <span class="material-symbols-outlined">graphic_eq</span> instead of its number.

The <span class="material-symbols-outlined">close</span> button at the end of a row takes that single
track out of the queue without changing what is playing.

Tracks are added to the queue from the library — **Add to queue** and **Play next** in the
[song options](/nextcp2/user_interface/music_library/#song-options), or the **PLAY** / **SHUFFLE**
buttons of a browse page, which replace the queue with what that page shows.

This page requires a UPnP media renderer. The built-in browser player (**This Device**) keeps its
queue inside the browser instead — see [Player Bar](/nextcp2/user_interface/player_bar/).
